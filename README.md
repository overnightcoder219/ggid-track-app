# GGID Track

GPS tracking and dashcam app for Android, for fleet customers of a GPS-tracking service. A live map of every vehicle with a fleet view and My location, device details with the address, live dashcam video (CH1/CH2) with sound, push-to-talk to the driver, SD-card playback and clips, photos and alarm snapshots, alerts with per-type settings and push notifications, and geofences. Follows the phone's light or dark mode. Accounts are created by your GPS provider; there is no self sign-up. Android 7 or newer.

- Website and setup guide: https://overnightcoder219.github.io/ggid-track-app/
- Download: [Releases](../../releases/latest). Each release has the APK and its SHA-256.
